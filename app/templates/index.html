Unfiltered archive

Where the original is copied before filtering. This copy is the only way back to the raw cut, since video cuts remove frames permanently. Keep it on the same volume as your media — a cross-volume archive is a full copy.

Library roots

Paths the library scanner walks. Inside Docker these are the container's mount points, e.g. /media/movies.
